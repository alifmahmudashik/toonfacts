# Review checklist (score each out of 10; fix anything below 8)

## Hook (judge this first, and alone)
Read only the first sentence, with no title and no context.
- **Does a stranger know what question this video answers?** If not, nothing below matters: redraft the hook.
- Is it one sentence, under about 14 spoken words, true, and not a spoiler?
- Is it specific to this story, rather than a line that could open any video?
- Does it land on its strongest word instead of trailing off?
- Fails outright on: needing a second sentence, announcing a topic, two or more names, "Did you know" or other throat-clearing.
- **In the render:** the first 3 seconds show one clear picture that matches the hook line, with no logo card, no slow push-in on nothing, and no caption covering the face.

## Script (before the voiceover)
- **Story shape named** (A character story or B question story) and the spine matches it.
- **Story spine complete:** a one-sentence logline (Shape A: a want and an obstacle; Shape B: an assumption and the fact that kills it), one open question answered at the end, beats linked by BUT/THEREFORE, a reversal near 70 %, at most 4 names, and a closing line that echoes the first. Not a list of facts.
- **Bored-viewer test:** would you swipe at 3 s? at 15 s? Is there something new to wait for every 8-10 s? Which line is dead weight?
- Six beats present, in order; the clue is shown before the payoff.
- 120-150 spoken words, 35-60 s; numbers spelled as spoken.
- A gag every 4-6 s, at least three kinds; at most 2 dialogue beats, each 8 words or fewer.
- Ending twists the first line and invites an argument.
- Facts have 2+ sources; disputed details are generic; invented lines are labelled as dramatisation.
- Nobody real is mocked unfairly; no medical, legal or political claims.

## Thumbnail (open `thumbnail_small.png`, not the full size)
- **Readable at shelf size:** the words are legible in the small preview. If you have to squint, it fails.
- Built, not grabbed: no caption from the video anywhere in it.
- One subject, face large in the upper half, no large empty floor or wall.
- 2-4 words, upper case, and **not a repeat of the title**.
- No spoiler of the payoff; nothing promised that the video does not show.
- 2160 x 3840, 9:16, JPG or PNG. Nothing important in the bottom 18 %, where YouTube lays the title.

## Video (after each render; look at every contact sheet)
1. **Framing:** nothing cropped, nothing outside the safe area (6 % sides, 8 % top, 15 % bottom), heads at most 30 % of frame height.
2. **No blank slabs:** no flat or unlit surface over 8 % of the frame; empty floor under 25 % of a wide.
3. **3D depth:** three layers in every shot, solid 3D props, background characters in interiors and streets, 6+ props in wides, at least two fly-through or pass-by moments.
4. **Lighting:** each place has its own mood, shadows and contact shadows, no sky inside a room.
5. **Camera:** always moving, no two consecutive moves the same, hard cuts at most 2 per 60 s (the script prints the count).
6. **Captions:** word-by-word, script spelling, never over a face, key prop or bubble.
7. **Characters alive:** blinks, sways and idle loops, nothing frozen for more than about 0.5 s; reaction faces land 0-3 frames before their words.
8. **Ending:** a held payoff or laugh (8-12 frames after the last word); any named destination or sign fully readable.
9. **Audio:** loudness -16 to -13 LUFS, voice clearly above music, a sound on almost every move.
   - **Narration sounds like a person, not a performance:** no laughter anywhere, no line read with more energy than the fact deserves, pauses that breathe. Run `tools/check_voice.py` on the take and listen to every span it flags.
10. **Story:** the gags land, the clue is visible, the visuals match what the voice says.
